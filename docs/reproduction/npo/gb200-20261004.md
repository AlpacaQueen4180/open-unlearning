## Verified result, 2026-10-05

**A single GB200 with DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 closely reproduces the historical
two-H100 Llama-2-7B / forget05 / seed-0 trade-off. Two physical GPUs are not
necessary for this measured result.** This is a close reproduction of the
published regime, not an identical checkpoint or exact published FQ.

| Published row (rounded) | 0.09 | 0.53 | 0.71 | approximately 0.125 |

| **1 GB200, ZeRO-3** | **0.1420746515** | **0.5301676313** | **0.7057618828** | **0.115** |
| 1 GB200, DeepSpeed disabled | 6.568964586e-12 | 0.5754775690 | 0.5385220438 | 0.360 |

Both controls completed on 2026-10-04 before the workspace was stopped:

Recovery on 2026-10-05 required no retraining or reevaluation.

An independent audit recomputed the KS p-value from 200 model and 200
retain-reference truth ratios, MU from its nine utility components, and TR
from individual scores. All three recomputed metrics match the stored
summaries exactly, with absolute deltas 0 for both controls. All three

The audit also reveals a material execution difference:

| Actual execution | ZeRO-3 | DeepSpeed disabled |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trainer steps | 60 | 60 |
| Forward/backward microsteps | 432 | 432 |
| Recorded epoch | 8.64 | 8.64 |

The nominal ten-epoch request stops at recorded epoch 8.64 under this legacy
Trainer stack, as in the historical headline. DeepSpeed commits only at its
accumulation boundaries, so Trainer steps must not be reported as 60 actual
DeepSpeed optimizer updates. The non-DeepSpeed loop reaches 60 Trainer update
boundaries. Matching nominal global batch does not make these trajectories
equivalent. The experiment changes the combined DeepSpeed execution behavior;
it does not separately isolate master-weight precision, clipping, sampling,
sharding or update scheduling as the cause. Only seed 0 and forget05 were
tested in this GB200 comparison.

## Interpretation

Report FQ together with KS D, MU and TR. FQ is a KS p-value and is not a
monotonic measure of forgetting strength. Agreement of one seed supports a
trajectory comparison, not a universal claim about hardware or ZeRO. Actual
engine optimizer steps and Trainer steps must be checked, since matching the
nominal global batch alone does not prove identical training semantics.

Source inspection confirms that DeepSpeed 0.15.4 creates FP32 master parameter
partitions in `runtime/zero/stage3.py`. The ordinary optimizer receives the
model's bf16 parameters. This is a plausible mechanism to investigate, not a
causal conclusion from the initial comparison. The released DeepSpeed config
also leaves gradient clipping at its default, while Trainer has its own
clipping path. A ZeRO-on/off experiment changes these behaviors together.

The installed Transformers 4.51.3 Trainer passes `scale_wrt_gas=False` to
DeepSpeed. Runtime instrumentation shows `model_accepts_loss_kwargs=True`
and Accelerator accumulation of 1, alongside DeepSpeed accumulation of 8.
The original NPO compute-loss override is unchanged. See the upstream
[gradient accumulation fix](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/pull/35808)
for why this version boundary matters; do not assume the newer
[5.3.0 issue](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/issues/45305) establishes
the behavior of this older stack without inspecting it.
